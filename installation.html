INSTALLATION GUIDE

Redmi / POCO / Xiaomi

Follow the instructions carefully before installing Redmi Custom OS on your supported device.

Brand Redmi / POCO / Xiaomi
Status Active
Release Monthly
Maintainer Redmi Custom OS Team

Instructions

Keep in mind...

  • Make sure your device is officially supported.
  • Take a complete backup of your important data.
  • Use the recovery recommended for your specific device.
  • Do not flash files made for a different device.
  • Always read the device-specific instructions before installing.

Clean Flash

A clean flash is recommended when coming from another ROM or when specifically required by the device maintainer.

  1. Download the correct ROM for your Redmi, POCO or Xiaomi device.
  2. Download the recovery recommended for your device.
  3. Backup your important files before continuing.
  4. Reboot your device into the required bootloader/recovery mode.
  5. Install the recommended recovery according to the device-specific guide.
  6. Format data if the device-specific instructions require it.
  7. Install the Redmi Custom OS ROM package.
  8. Reboot the device after installation is complete.

Dirty Flash / Update

Dirty flashing may be possible when updating from a compatible Redmi Custom OS build. Always keep a backup before updating.

  1. Download the latest ROM build for your exact device.
  2. Reboot into the recovery recommended for your device.
  3. Install the new ROM package.
  4. Reboot your device.

⚠ Important

Installation procedures can differ between Redmi, POCO and Xiaomi devices. Always use the instructions provided for your exact device and codename.

Redmi Custom OS is not responsible for data loss or damage caused by incorrect installation.
